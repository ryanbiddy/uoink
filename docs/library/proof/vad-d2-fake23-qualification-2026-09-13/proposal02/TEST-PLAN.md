# Focused23 adapter controls

These23 methods are copied byte-for-byte from proposal01, SHA-256 a106a62d095d77331c6ebb0ded07e588a9d80c632930325c033c4d185e2b7fd8. No case has run. EXPECTED-CASES.json records their sorted names; the qualifier verifies exact membership and uses ordinary unittest setup/teardown and assertions.

Coverage is the closed public entry before converter/evidence access; fixed evidence preservation and changed-byte refusal; absent/wrong owner digest; explicit scope, interpretation, migration and notice prerequisites; refusal of broader reader/model/network/release claims; required source-note/text; duplicate/oversized JSON; false/wrong root admission; private fake-port parameter delivery; existing profile and wrong path/plan refusal; exact error propagation/reset on write/deadline failures; replaced profile; and malformed or overclaiming conversion reports.

Positive generated decisions and admissions are private validator inputs. They are not Ryan records and cannot activate run_once while OWNER_DECISION_SHA256 is None. FakeConverter supplies a record-only stand-in for the port; its write/deadline controls raise generated errors and verify propagation/reset. They do not exercise a filesystem write or the real converter's deadline. The four evidence files are previously reviewed text; no source storage bytes are read.

The qualifier's fixed module allowlist excludes fixed_converter, zip_bounds, d2_child, launch_d2 and all named ML/pickle roots. Existing test bodies, assertions and the frozen converter/plan remain unchanged. A future valid23 result would establish only these adapter contracts. The reporting repair and real child/parent remain subject to separate source review and any specifically required new boundary qualification.
