The taxonomy proposal must include:

- For every new concept, at least five distinct supporting induction cards with source/card/excerpt IDs and a verbatim quote of 1–24 words from each. A card can support more than one concept, but repeated excerpts from one item count once. Titles, summaries and model-generated abstention reasons cannot supply that support.

Remove the old prompt's forced number of top shelves/sub-shelves when the evidence cannot support it; do not invent children or a miscellaneous shelf to meet a shape target.

Strengthen assignment rules at both the prompt and validation boundaries.
