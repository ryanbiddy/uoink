Browser button

Get the extension

The Uoink button lives in a browser extension you load once. It's already on this machine at %LOCALAPPDATA%\Uoink\extension.

  1. Open your extensions page: chrome://extensions (Chrome), edge://extensions (Edge), or brave://extensions (Brave).
  2. Turn on Developer mode in the top right.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the extension folder that just opened.

Capture

Auto-uoink playlists

Checking playlist monitor...

Taste-aware auto-uoink

Off by default. When on, Uoink scores new videos from the playlists you already monitor (above) against your taste — your 10/10 anchors, admired channels, engagement, and corpus — and auto-captures the strong matches. They land in your library labelled auto-uoinked (taste match).

Checking auto-uoink status...

Only works over sources you already track — no web crawling, no new accounts. Capture uses the same local download + transcription as a manual save, so there is no AI spend. Reversible: turn it off any time, and auto-captured uoinks are ordinary uoinks you can delete.

Podcast feeds

Checking podcast feeds...

Feed watching saves metadata only by default. Auto-ingest uses the selected local transcription model; a first-time model download still needs approval.

Sites Uoink works on

Checking site permissions...

Uoink only works on sites you've enabled. Right-click any page and choose "Uoink this page"; if the site needs permission, the extension asks first.

Transcript checker

Checking transcript checker...

Runs locally. When captions are missing, the selected model can take about 10–15 minutes per hour of video on a typical laptop; larger models may take longer. Downloads only after you ask and stays in your Uoink data folder.

AI features

AI writing key

Checking key status...

Stored on this device and used only for the optional AI features you turn on.

Claim verification

When on, every uoink's transcript is scanned for checkable factual claims. Evidence is shown for your judgment. Uoink never auto-asserts truth.

Entity extraction

Pull people, tools, products and companies off each transcript into the library graph. Uses your Anthropic key (~2,500 output tokens per item). Off by default; a saved key alone never starts it.

Writing Studio

Library Mirror

Optional one-way export of evidence cards, shelf pages, and accepted briefs into <vault>/Uoink/. Off by default. Enabling or broadening scope needs a preview and a confirmation from this page.

Other software with access to that vault can index its contents. Uoink does not register the vault with Basic Memory/Hermes, start another indexer, edit its configuration, turn on sync, or infer consent from an installed application. Existing third-party indexing is outside Uoink's deletion control.

Standing capture consent
Enable standing capture?

  • Back-catalog policy: Up to 25 most recent items will be enrolled on the first valid snapshot. Older items remain metadata-only.
  • Daily allowance: Up to 10 starts per UTC day across this source. Unused capacity resets at 00:00 UTC.
  • Standing ingest: Audio/media download and local transcription begin on the next scheduler tick; unfiled captures land in your Library.
